Welcome to NeoCMS 3

A database-free CMS with drafts, revisions, scheduled publishing, and rather fewer excuses for editing live HTML.

Edit and publish

Open Pages, choose a page, and select any region carrying the configured editable class.

Save Draft keeps work private. Publish creates a revision before replacing the public page.

Pages and revisions

Administrators can create, duplicate, rename, and delete pages. Publishers can restore earlier revisions from Tools.

Deleted pages retain a restorable snapshot, because accidents should be reversible rather than memorable.

Media and SEO

The media library reuses uploaded images, records alternative text, and reports usage counts.

The SEO dialogue manages titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph images, and indexing instructions.

Shared content and menus

Regions marked with data-neo-shared can be updated across every page. Named menus may be nested and propagated in the same fashion.

Schedule and preview

Publishers may queue a future release. Desktop, tablet, and mobile controls resize the preview without altering page markup.

Checks and activity

The accessibility checker catches common authoring problems before publication. The dashboard lists drafts, schedules, recent activity, and writable-directory warnings.

NeoCMS 3 documentation is available in the project README.
